Beloved — Safety protocol

Last updated: November 2, 2026 · Published under California SB 243 and New York's AI companion law.

AI disclosure

Every story starts with a notice that the characters are AI and the story is fiction. If you sincerely ask a character whether it is an AI, it says so plainly.

Self-harm and suicide

Messages that express thoughts of suicide or self-harm are detected on your iPhone, in every language Beloved is offered in, before they are sent. Beloved immediately shows crisis resources for where you are — for example 988 in the US, 109 in Korea, 116 123 in the UK and Poland, 0800 111 0 111 in Germany, 3114 in France, 024 in Spain, 188 in Brazil — and findahelpline.com elsewhere. The character steps out of the story right away, without the usual reply delay, asks whether you are safe and names the same line.

Characters never encourage self-harm.

Breaks

After three hours of continuous reading, Beloved suggests a break and reminds you that characters are AI.

Adults only

Beloved is for adults 18+, with an age check at the start. Sexual content involving minors is blocked on input and output. Explicit sexual content is not written; scenes fade to black.

Private details

Phone numbers, emails, addresses, national ID and card numbers are blocked before sending and masked in replies.

Reporting

Report a character or a reply from the story menu, or email borabada77@gmail.com. We respond within 48 hours.
