Beloved — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Beloved has no accounts, no ads SDK and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Your stories

  • Your series progress, messages, memories (“keepsakes”), characters you create or import, and settings are stored on your iPhone and in its iCloud Backup. We have no server that stores them.
  • You can export everything or delete everything in You → Your data.

The AI that writes replies

  • Episodes are written in advance. When you text a character yourself, the recent messages of that chat, the series and character description, your first name and the memories relevant to that reply are sent to the AI service that runs the language model, so it can write the reply. You agree to this before your first story.
  • The service is currently DeepSeek (api.deepseek.com, operated by Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd.; its servers may be outside your country, including in China). If you connect your own key in You → AI connection, replies go to the service you choose, such as OpenRouter.
  • These messages are used only to write the reply. They are not sold and not used for advertising.
  • When replies go through Beloved's own relay, the relay passes the messages on without storing them. It keeps a random install number and that day's reply count for two days to apply the daily limit, and, if you have the Pass, asks Apple whether it is active.
  • Before anything is sent, Beloved blocks personal details on your iPhone: phone numbers, email addresses, street addresses, national ID and card numbers never leave the device. Replies are checked the same way on the way back.

Local weather and news in stories

So characters can mention what is really happening in their city, the app fetches the current weather (Open-Meteo) and public news headlines (Google News search) for the series' city, for example Seoul or New York. These requests contain only the city and search words, never anything about you. Results are cached on your iPhone for a few hours. Headlines about tragedies, crime and politics are filtered out.

Notifications

Story texts, calls, replies that arrive later and the trial reminder are scheduled locally on your iPhone if you allow notifications.

Age

Beloved is for adults 18 and over. We ask for your birth year once and keep only the year, on your iPhone.

Purchases

The Beloved Pass and coin packs are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Your coins, daily ticket and reply count are kept on your iPhone.

Your rights

Because your stories stay on your iPhone, you can see, export and delete them yourself at any time. For any other request about your data, write to us and we will answer within 30 days.
