Beloved

Drama series you live on your own phone.

Fake marriages, mafia oaths, rejected mates, a divorce he regrets. In Beloved every series plays out in your messages: texts, group chats, calls that ring, choices that change who falls for you. And between episodes, you can text them back, and they answer in character.

  • 12 original series, new episodes you open one at a time. Episodes 1 and 2 are always free.
  • One free ticket every day opens the next episode of any series.
  • Your choices count: who you trust, what you say, and how the season ends.
  • His side of the story, secret scenes and special choices, each with its price on the button.
  • The Beloved Pass opens every episode, unlimited replies and 20 coins a day.
  • In English, 한국어, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Polski and Português.

For adults 18+. Characters are AI and everything they say is fiction.
