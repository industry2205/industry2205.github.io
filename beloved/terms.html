Beloved — Terms of Use

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Beloved is interactive fiction for adults 18+. Characters are artificial intelligence. Nothing they say is professional, medical, legal or financial advice.

Don't use Beloved to create content involving minors, non-consent, hate, or real people without their consent. Characters you create stay private on your iPhone.

Episodes and coins

  • Episodes 1 and 2 of every series are free. Each day you get one free ticket that opens the next episode of any series. An episode can also be opened with 30 coins.
  • Extras show their price on the button: his or her side of the story (20 coins), a secret scene (45 coins), a special choice (35 coins, always next to a free choice). Redoing an AI reply is free.
  • Coin packs are consumable in-app purchases. Coins never expire, are kept on your iPhone and have no cash value.

Beloved Pass

The Beloved Pass is an auto-renewing subscription: weekly, monthly, or yearly with a 7-day free trial. It opens every episode, gives unlimited replies (fair use) and 20 coins each day while it is active. Payment is charged to your Apple Account; it renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Without the Pass, you can send 50 replies a day, resetting at local midnight.
