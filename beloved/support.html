Beloved — Support

How do episodes open?

Episodes 1 and 2 are free. After that, use your free daily ticket (it refills at midnight), 30 coins, or the Beloved Pass, which opens everything.

Can I change a choice?

Yes. Right after a choice, tap Rewind that choice (15 coins, free with the Pass) and choose again.

He forgot something.

Open What they remember in the phone menu. Add it, or pin it so it's always on their mind. You can also long-press any message and choose Remember this.

How do I bring my characters from Character.AI?

In Character.AI, request your data (Settings → Account → Manage Account & Data). When the email arrives, save the .zip to Files, then in Beloved tap Bring characters from Character.AI at the bottom of For You and choose the file. Tavern/SillyTavern cards (.png or .json) work too.

How do I cancel the Pass?

Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Beloved.

Where are my stories?

On your iPhone and in its iCloud Backup. Export them anytime in You → Your data.
